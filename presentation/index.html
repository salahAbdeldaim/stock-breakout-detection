Breakout Event Definition (Day 0)
A breakout is triggered strictly when:
1. Close > max(High of previous 30 sessions)
2. Volume ≥ 1.5 × SMA20(Volume)

Phase 2: Mathematical Rigor

Mathematical Formulations & Rationale

Deep dive into the core mathematical formulations driving signal generation and collinearity elimination.

Institutional Accumulation Formula
Volume_Surge_10:
$$Volume\_Surge\_10 = \frac{Volume_{t_0}}{\frac{1}{10} \sum_{i=1}^{10} Volume_{t_0 - i}}$$
Financial Intuition: Genuine institutional breakouts require massive liquidity absorption. A value > 2.5 indicates that institutional desks are participating, whereas a value < 1.2 suggests an unsupported retail pump.
Intraday Rejection Filter
Close_Position:
$$Close\_Position = \frac{Close_{t_0} - Low_{t_0}}{High_{t_0} - Low_{t_0}}$$
Financial Intuition: If a stock breaks out but closes near its low (Close_Position < 0.50), it forms a massive upper rejection wick. Smart money distributed their shares at the high, leaving retail trapped.
Volatility Normalization
ATR_Pct:
$$ATR\_Pct = \frac{ATR_{14}(t_0)}{Close_{t_0}} \times 100$$
Financial Intuition: Normalizes price action across high-beta equities (e.g. AMD, TSLA) and low-beta defensives (e.g. JNJ, PG), preventing false breakout triggers during volatile market-wide regime shifts.
Collinearity Elimination Audit
Eliminated Redundancy:
Breakout_Pct ≡ Resistance_Distance_% (Pearson r = 1.000)
Engineering Audit: In our dataset cleanup phase, we identified that Breakout_Pct was a 100% duplicate alias of Resistance_Distance_%. We completely purged Breakout_Pct across all notebooks and unified datasets, restoring numerical stability.

Phase 7: Interpretability & Governance

Explainable AI via TreeSHAP

Eliminating the black-box stigma through exact Shapley additive feature attributions (Lundberg & Lee, 2017).

Additive Attribution Formulation
f(x) = φ0 +
M ∑ j=1
φj(x)
$$f(x) = \phi_0 + \sum_{j=1}^M \phi_j(x)$$
φ0 (Base Value): Baseline expected breakout probability across the training distribution.
φj(x) (Shapley Value): Exact marginal attribution added or subtracted by feature j.
M: Total dimensional feature space (M = 16 engineered alpha drivers).
Bifurcated Driver Interpretation
Green Drivers (Conviction): Features pushing the prediction higher toward confirmed breakout (e.g. Volume Surge > 2.5x, Close Position > 0.90).

Red Drivers (Risk Drag): Features dragging the prediction down toward fakeout (e.g. high ATR expansion, excessive MA divergence).
Regulatory Compliance & Institutional Defense
In institutional finance, a black-box model is unacceptable to risk officers and regulators (SEC / FINRA). TreeSHAP guarantees full auditability: for every trade executed or rejected, StockPred outputs a deterministic mathematical receipt explaining precisely why the committee acted.
1. Explain the importance of TreeSHAP: Quantitative funds cannot deploy models that fund managers cannot explain to risk committees or regulators.
2. Demystify the math: Every trade has a base probability $\phi_0$. Features either add positive conviction (green) or subtract risk drag (red). Nothing is hidden.
